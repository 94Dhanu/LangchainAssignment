EMPLOYEE BENEFITS POLICY

1. Health Insurance
All full-time employees and their dependents (spouse and up to two children) are covered
by the company group health insurance plan with an annual coverage limit of 500,000 USD.
Coverage begins on the first day of employment.

2. Retirement Plan
The company contributes 6% of the employee's base salary to the retirement savings plan.
Employees may make additional voluntary contributions. Company contributions vest fully
after 3 years of service.

3. Learning and Development
Each employee receives an annual learning budget of 1,500 USD for courses,
certifications, and conferences. Approval from the reporting manager is required.
Exam fees for job-related certifications are reimbursed 100% on passing.

4. Employee Assistance Program
Confidential counseling services are available 24/7 to employees and their families for
personal, financial, and mental health matters at no cost.

5. Gym and Wellness
Employees are reimbursed up to 50 USD per month for gym memberships or wellness
programs upon submission of receipts.

6. Referral Bonus
Employees who refer a candidate who is hired and completes 6 months of service receive
a referral bonus of 1,000 USD.

7. Meal and Transport Allowance
A daily meal allowance of 8 USD is provided on working days. Employees commuting by
public transport receive a monthly transport allowance of 60 USD.
