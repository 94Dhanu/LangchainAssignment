WORK FROM HOME (WFH) POLICY

1. Eligibility
Employees who have completed 6 months of service and hold roles that can be performed
remotely are eligible for work from home. Customer-facing and lab-based roles require
on-site presence and are not eligible.

2. Hybrid Schedule
The company follows a hybrid model. Eligible employees may work from home up to
3 days per week. Employees must be present in the office at least 2 days per week,
on days decided by the team.

3. Request Process
WFH days must be marked in the HRMS portal at least one day in advance. Occasional
full-remote weeks require manager approval. Employees on probation may work from
home at most 1 day per week.

4. Availability
During WFH, employees must be reachable on company chat and email during core hours
(11:00 AM to 4:00 PM) and must attend all scheduled meetings with video enabled
unless otherwise agreed with the manager.

5. Equipment and Internet
The company provides a laptop and a one-time home-office setup allowance of Rs 15,000.
A monthly internet reimbursement of Rs 1,000 is paid to employees who WFH at least
8 days per month. Employees are responsible for maintaining a reliable connection.

6. Security Requirements
When working remotely, employees must connect through the company VPN, keep the
endpoint security agent running, and must not store confidential data on personal
devices or public cloud storage.
