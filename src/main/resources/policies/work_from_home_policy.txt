WORK FROM HOME POLICY

1. Eligibility
Employees who have completed at least 6 months of service and whose roles can be
performed remotely are eligible for work from home (WFH). Eligibility is determined by
the reporting manager and HR.

2. WFH Allowance
Eligible employees may work from home up to 2 days per week under the hybrid work
model. Fully remote arrangements require approval from the department head.

3. Availability
During WFH days, employees must be reachable on company chat and email during core
hours (10:30 AM to 4:30 PM) and must attend all scheduled meetings by video call.

4. Equipment and Internet
The company provides a laptop and a monthly internet allowance of 40 USD for approved
WFH employees. Employees are responsible for a secure workspace and a reliable
internet connection of at least 25 Mbps.

5. Security Requirements
Employees must use the company VPN when accessing internal systems, keep devices
encrypted and locked when unattended, and must not use public Wi-Fi without VPN.

6. Requesting WFH
WFH days must be requested in the HR portal at least one day in advance and approved
by the reporting manager. WFH requests can be revoked during critical business periods.
