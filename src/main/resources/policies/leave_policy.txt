COMPANY LEAVE POLICY

1. Annual Leave
All full-time employees are entitled to 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
Annual leave accrues at a rate of 1.67 days per month of service. New employees may use
accrued leave after completing 90 days of service. Up to 5 unused annual leave days may
be carried over into the next calendar year; carried-over days must be used by March 31.

2. Sick Leave
Employees receive 12 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required
for absences longer than 2 consecutive days. Unused sick leave does not carry over and
is not paid out.

3. Casual Leave
Employees are entitled to 7 days of casual leave per year for urgent personal matters.
Casual leave must normally be approved by the reporting manager in advance, except in
emergencies, where it must be reported on the same day.

4. Maternity and Paternity Leave
Eligible employees receive 26 weeks of paid maternity leave. Paternity leave is 15 days
of paid leave, which must be used within 6 months of the child's birth or adoption.

5. Leave Without Pay
Leave without pay may be granted for up to 30 days per year at the discretion of the
department head and HR. Benefits do not accrue during leave without pay.

6. Applying for Leave
Leave requests must be submitted through the HR portal at least 3 working days in
advance for planned leave. Approval is granted by the reporting manager and visible in
the HR portal.
