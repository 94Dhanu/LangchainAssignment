COMPANY ATTENDANCE POLICY

1. Working Hours
Standard working hours are 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday through Friday, with a one-hour
lunch break. Employees are expected to complete 9 working hours per day and 45 hours
per week.

2. Flexi-Time
The company offers a flexi-time window: employees may start work any time between
8:00 AM and 10:30 AM, provided they complete their daily hours and are available
during core hours (11:00 AM to 4:00 PM).

3. Attendance Marking
Attendance must be recorded through the biometric system or the HRMS mobile app when
entering and leaving the office. Missing a punch must be regularized through the
HRMS portal within 3 working days; a maximum of 4 regularizations per month is allowed.

4. Late Arrival
Arriving after 10:30 AM more than 3 times in a month is treated as a half-day leave.
Habitual late arrival may result in disciplinary action.

5. Overtime
Work beyond standard hours requires prior approval from the reporting manager.
Non-managerial employees are eligible for compensatory time off for approved overtime.
Comp off must be claimed within 60 days.

6. Holidays
The company observes 10 national and regional holidays per year, announced at the
start of each calendar year. Employees working on a declared holiday are entitled
to a compensatory day off.
