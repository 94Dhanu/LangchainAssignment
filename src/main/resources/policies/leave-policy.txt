COMPANY LEAVE POLICY

1. Annual Leave
All full-time employees are entitled to 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
Leave is accrued at a rate of 2 days per month of service. New employees may use accrued
leave only after completing 3 months of service.

2. Sick Leave
Employees are entitled to 12 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is
required for sick leave exceeding 2 consecutive days. Unused sick leave does not carry
over to the next calendar year.

3. Carry Forward
A maximum of 5 unused annual leave days may be carried forward to the next calendar year.
Carried-forward leave must be used within the first quarter (January to March) or it
will lapse automatically.

4. Leave Application Process
Employees must apply for planned leave through the HRMS portal at least 3 working days
in advance. Leave requests require approval from the reporting manager. For leave longer
than 5 consecutive days, additional approval from the department head is required.

5. Emergency Leave
In case of a family or medical emergency, employees may take emergency leave by informing
their manager on the same day. Emergency leave is deducted from the annual leave balance.

6. Maternity and Paternity Leave
Female employees are entitled to 26 weeks of paid maternity leave. Male employees are
entitled to 15 days of paid paternity leave, which must be availed within 6 months of
the child's birth.

7. Leave Without Pay
Employees who have exhausted their paid leave balance may request leave without pay,
subject to manager and HR approval. Leave without pay is limited to 30 days per year.
