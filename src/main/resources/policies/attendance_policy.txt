COMPANY ATTENDANCE POLICY

1. Working Hours
Standard working hours are 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday through Friday, with a one-hour
lunch break. Employees are expected to complete 8 working hours per day and 40 hours
per week.

2. Flex Time
Employees may start work between 8:00 AM and 10:30 AM and adjust their end time
accordingly, provided the full 8 hours are completed and core hours (10:30 AM to
4:30 PM) are covered.

3. Attendance Recording
Attendance is recorded through the biometric system or the mobile attendance app.
Employees must punch in and punch out daily. Missed punches must be regularized in the
HR portal within 3 working days.

4. Late Arrival
Arriving after 10:30 AM without prior approval is marked as a late arrival. Three late
arrivals in a month are counted as half a day of casual leave. Repeated tardiness may
lead to disciplinary action.

5. Overtime
Overtime requires prior written approval from the reporting manager. Eligible employees
are compensated at 1.5 times the hourly rate or may opt for compensatory time off within
60 days.

6. Absence Without Notice
An absence of 3 or more consecutive days without notice may be treated as job
abandonment and can result in termination of employment.
