EMPLOYEE BENEFITS POLICY

1. Health Insurance
All full-time employees are covered under the group health insurance plan with a
sum insured of Rs 5,00,000 per year. Coverage extends to the employee, spouse, and
up to 2 dependent children at no extra cost. Parents can be added for an
additional premium of Rs 12,000 per year.

2. Provident Fund
The company contributes 12% of the basic salary to the Employees' Provident Fund,
matched by an equal employee contribution as per statutory requirements.

3. Learning and Development
Each employee has an annual learning budget of Rs 25,000 for courses, certifications,
conferences, and books. Certification exam fees are reimbursed in full on passing.
Employees must complete at least 24 hours of learning per year.

4. Meal and Travel Benefits
Employees working from the office receive meal vouchers worth Rs 2,200 per month.
Employees commuting to the office may claim fuel or public-transport reimbursement
up to Rs 3,000 per month against receipts.

5. Employee Assistance Program
Free, confidential counselling services are available 24x7 for employees and their
immediate family members covering mental health, legal, and financial matters.

6. Performance Bonus
Employees are eligible for an annual performance bonus of 8% to 15% of the annual
base salary depending on individual performance ratings and company results.
